On the likeness (EMH.md section 8): he is the Emergency Medical Hologram, a
descriptive designation, not a named character. The prompt describes a
uniform and a stance, never a person, and the badge is deliberately a blank
oval -- the first generation returned a Starfleet delta and was thrown away
for it. Nothing here is derived from a frame of anything.
