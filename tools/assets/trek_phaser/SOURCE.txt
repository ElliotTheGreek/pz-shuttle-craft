These two files are the vendored source: tools/gen_phaser.py reads them and
needs no network and no package beyond PIL and numpy. They were made once by
tools/bake_phaser.py from an image-to-3D raw, which is NOT committed (18 MB;
.gitignore) and is recorded here instead. Re-running the pipeline below gives
a *different* phaser, not the same one again.

Also generated from the same concept and seed, and rejected: fal-ai/trellis
(v1), request 01a0d5a9-9da8-7e20-acb7-054253991784, 8444 faces. Its shape was
as good, but it invented a cyan lens on the back and orange speckle on the
grip, and v2's geometry was cleaner. Both texture faults would have been
painted out by the bake anyway; v2 won on geometry.

The concept prompt (three-quarter view), in full:

  Product design render of an original fictional late-24th-century starship
  crew's handheld energy phaser pistol, three-quarter view from the
  front-left and slightly above, the whole object centered and fully in
  frame with generous margin, on a plain pure white seamless background,
  soft even studio lighting, no shadow on the floor, no text, no logos, no
  insignia, no hands. Design: compact and sleek, about the size of a TV
  remote with a pistol grip. The upper body is a smooth tapered wedge in
  matte warm-grey/off-white composite, narrowing toward the front, ending in
  a slightly recessed round emitter lens that glows soft orange. A satin
  black pistol grip angles down and back under the rear of the body, with
  subtle finger ridges. A thin dark grey seam runs along the flank, a small
  row of four tiny blue indicator lights on the side, and a small dark
  rectangular setting control on top near the back. Rounded edges, clean
  readable silhouette, simple large shapes, game-asset style, physically
  plausible.

What the raw needed, all of it done by tools/bake_phaser.py and all of it
found on design/art/weapons/phaser/phaser_sheet.png rather than in the
numbers (PHASERS.md 4 has the story):

  - it is a hollow shell with a dark inner skin a millimetre inside the
    outer one, plus 1,046 loose scraps; decimated as it stood, the inner
    skin showed through as black triangles all over the body. Rebuilt from
    a filled volume (voxels, marching cubes) into one closed skin;
  - its texture is darker than the concept, lit, and has TRELLIS's own
    inventions (a dark emitter, an orange patch on the back); repainted
    per texel in the concept's palette, the emitter found by geometry;
  - true to the concept it is 0.041 across, twice vanilla's M9; slimmed to
    0.030, inside vanilla's pistol bracket;
  - it is fitted grip-to-grip onto vanilla's M9 (the mesh behind Handgun03),
    so Handgun03's own hand and ground attachments fit it.

The four blue indicator lights in the concept did not survive TRELLIS at all
(the bake finds no blue in the raw's texture). At the size a phaser is drawn
in a hand they would be one pixel. Not painted in; say so if they are wanted.
