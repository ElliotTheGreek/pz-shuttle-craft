-> vulcan 2, light 2, water 1

= water

    | Maybe the auto-feed's drowning them.
bot: That's the other thing. They're used to working for it.
    | They grow in dry ground. Hourly water spoils them.

    | So they're sulking because life is too easy.

    | Every plant on this deck has a temperament. These have two.
-> vulcan 2, pass_in 2

    | Help me reset the panel timers on row four.

    | Every hour we give the plomeek comes off somebody else.

-> pass_in 2, vulcan 1

    | She said her mother's were taller. Then she apologised for saying it.

    | That's a glowing review from a Vulcan.

    | Replicated plomeek tastes of the pattern buffer, she says.

    | Then we'd better not lose this row.

= pass_in

    | Is that the plomeek? It looks sad.

    | Row four has feelings.

    | Somebody played them Klingon opera in April. They've never recovered.

    | I'll hold you to that.
