# The field office on the ground (FIELD_STATION.md 2), as the Adirondack's crew
# talk about it. It went dark on 29 June; nobody aboard knows that anybody is
# alive down there. This is how a player hears that it exists and where: under
# the electronics shop at Dixie Highway and South Main, in Muldraugh, through
# the stockroom, behind the breaker box. Spread across the ship on purpose,
# and weighted to come up often, because otherwise nobody will ever find it.

# ---------------------------------------------------------------------------
scene: fo_lounge_theory
where: lounge, galley
cast: a=any, b=any
weight: 4

= start
a: Do you ever think about the field office? The one in Muldraugh?
 | I dreamed about the Muldraugh field office again.
 | Somebody should go and look at the field office. I mean it.
b: The TV shop? Dixie Highway and South Main? | Under the electronics shop?
 | The one that went dark in June?
a: That one. Eight people and a masking field, and not a word since.
 | Eight of the eleven worked out of there. Eight.
-> alive 2, access 2, sad 1

= alive
b: You think they're alive. | You think they're still down there.
a: I think a bunker under a shop is the best place in that county to be.
 | Sealed, powered, hidden. If I'd been down there, I'd still be down there.
b: Then why don't they answer? | Then why the silence?
a: Because the field hides them from everyone. Us included. That's its job.
 | Because somebody sabotaged us from inside. Would you call us?
-> close

= access
b: Could you even get in? It's a shop. With the county the way it is.
 | How would anybody get in, even if they went?
a: Front door on the lot side, through the shop, into the stockroom.
 | Walk in off South Main, through the shop floor, back to the stockroom.
b: And then? | Then what? It's a stockroom.
a: The breaker box on the stockroom wall. It isn't a breaker box.
 | West wall of the stockroom. Grey breaker box. Open it.
b: That's it? That's the security? | A breaker box. Shepard's idea?
a: Shepard's idea. Who looks twice at a breaker box?
 | Shepard. She said nobody in 1993 opens a breaker box for fun.
-> close

= sad
b: Don't. | I try not to think about it.
a: I know. I can't help it. Eight people, a mile from where we beam down.
 | Sorry. It's just... they're right there. Dixie and South Main.
b: If they were alive, they'd have called. | Maybe. Maybe not.
-> close

= close
a: Someone should just go and look. | One of these days somebody will.
b: Tell that to the captain. | Not me. I don't do stairs, let alone zombies.
 | If you find a way down, I'll come.
-> end

# ---------------------------------------------------------------------------
scene: fo_bridge_scan
where: bridge
cast: a=sciences, b=command
weight: 3

= start
a: Sir, I ran another passive sweep over Muldraugh.
 | Muldraugh sweep's done, {b}.
b: And? | Anything? | Same as yesterday?
a: The usual dead spot. Dixie Highway and South Main. Nothing reads there.
 | A hole in the sensors under the electronics shop. Same size as ever.
-> field 2, report 2

= field
b: That's the masking field. The field office's. It means the power is on.
 | A hole means the field's up. A field needs power. Power needs somebody.
a: Or it's on automatic and running on its last crystal.
 | Or the generator doesn't know everybody's gone.
b: ... Log it. Same as every day. | Log it. And keep looking.
-> end

= report
b: Put it in the report. Mark the shop. | Flag it on the survey map again.
a: It's flagged. It's been flagged since June. | Done. Stockroom entrance and all.
b: Then flag it louder. | Good. Somebody might need it one day.
-> end

where: transporter
cast: a=operations, b=any
weight: 3

= start
a: Can't lock onto it. Never could. | That's the field office again. No lock.
b: The Muldraugh one? | Under the shop? | Why not?
a: The masking field. It eats a transporter lock like it eats everything.
 | Their masking field. From up here it's a hole in the ground.

 | Dixie Highway and South Main. Breaker box in the stockroom. Their lift.

= brave
b: Anybody brave enough to walk in there deserves a medal.
 | Whoever does that is braver than me.

 | Whoever it is, I'd like to meet them.
-> end

= end_it
b: I hope somebody does. | One day.
a: Me too. | Every day I hope it.
-> end
