# Pattern-buffer fussiness.

scene: tr_buffer_fuss
where: transporter
cast: chief=operations, other=any
weight: 3

= start
chief: Don't lean on that. | Please don't put your coffee on the console.
     | Hands off the pad rail, if you would. Just calibrated it.
other: I wasn't touching it. | Sorry. It's a railing. People lean on railings.
     | It's a mug. It's got a lid.
chief: It's a transporter. People lean on it, people end up in Kansas.
     | That console holds eleven thousand terabytes of somebody's atoms.
-> buffer 3, cal 2, kess 1

= buffer
other: Is something wrong with it? | You're very tense this morning.
chief: Buffer's running a point-oh-three percent drift. Point-oh-three.
     | The pattern buffer hums half a tone flat. You can't hear it. I can.
other: Is that bad? | Is that dangerous? | Is that a lot?
chief: It's inside tolerance. Well inside. That's not the point.
     | By the book, no. By me, yes.
chief: Somebody comes up through this buffer with their nose a millimetre left.
     | You'd notice. Every mirror, for the rest of your life, you'd notice.
other: I'd think it was my face. | I wouldn't notice. My nose is already left.
-> fix 2, why 2

= cal
chief: Calibration. Third time this shift. | Running the Heisenberg check.
other: Third time? | Why three times?
chief: Once for the book. Once for the captain. Once for me.
     | Because the first two were for other people.
other: Does the third one ever find anything? | And does the third one differ?
chief: Once. In April. A phase variance in the secondary coil.
     | Never. That's why I trust it.
-> why 2, fix 1

= kess
chief: Kess wants the whole system certified before tonight's window.
     | Lieutenant th'Zoran's schedule has a beam at nineteen hundred.
other: Kess wants everything certified. | Kess would certify the air.
chief: Kess is right. Kess is always right. It's exhausting.
     | Andorians. They say what they think. Usually that you're late.
other: Is there a beam tonight? | Who's coming up?
chief: Maybe. A beacon's live. If they get to it. | If somebody down there can.
-> why 2, fix 1

= why
chief: This thing is all we've got. You know that?
     | No warp. No impulse. No comms. This. This room. That's it.
chief: Everyone who comes home, comes home through here.
     | Every one of the eleven that's come back, came back on that pad.
other: I know. | I hadn't thought of it that way. | That's a lot of weight.
chief: So I fuss. | So, yes. I'm going to fuss. I'll fuss all day.
-> spared 2, scott 1, fix 1, rhel 1

= spared
other: Funny, isn't it. Whoever broke the ship left this alone.
     | Why leave us the transporters? If you wanted us stuck?
chief: I think about that every shift. | I've asked myself a thousand times.
chief: Maybe they needed it. Maybe they didn't think it mattered.
     | Maybe they wanted us able to watch. And unable to leave.
other: That's a horrible thought. | I wish I hadn't asked.
chief: It's working, that's what counts. And I'm keeping it working.
     | They made a mistake. This room is their mistake. I like that.
-> fix 2, rhel 1

= scott
other: Didn't someone survive in a buffer once? For decades?
     | Wasn't there an engineer who lived in a pattern buffer?
chief: Montgomery Scott. Seventy-five years, on a loop. Old history now.
     | Captain Scott. Jury-rigged a buffer and came out seventy years later.
other: Could you do that? | Could we?
chief: With this buffer? Four seconds and I'm sweating.
     | Don't give Rhel ideas. He'd try it with the core.
-> fix

= rhel
other: Rhel could look at it. | Why not get engineering up here?
chief: Rhel's got a warp core to apologise to. I can handle a buffer.
     | Rhel came up once. Spent an hour telling the coils they were brave.
other: Did it help? | Were they? | And?
chief: The drift went down. I don't want to talk about it.
     | They've been very steady since. Don't tell him.
-> fix

= fix
...
chief: There. Point-oh-one. | Aligned. Listen. That's the right note.
     | Better. Point-oh-one. I'll take that.
other: I can't hear a difference. | Sounds exactly the same.
     | Congratulations. I think.
chief: You'd hear it if you were on the pad. | That's because it's right now.
other: Do you ever sleep? | When did you last take a day off?
chief: I sleep. In that chair. With the beacon alarm on my wrist.
     | Tuesdays. Kess makes me. I spend it thinking about the buffer.
-> leave 2, stay 1

= stay
other: Mind if I stay and watch? | Can I watch the next check?
chief: Stand there. Not there. There. Don't touch anything.
     | If you're quiet. And the coffee goes outside.
other: I'll hold very still. | Coffee's gone. I'm a statue.
-> end

= leave
other: I'll leave you to it. | I'll take my coffee somewhere safer.
chief: Thank you. | Mind the rail on your way out. | Go on. Shoo.
-> end

# A rescue arrives: a shaken ensign from the surface.

where: transporter
cast: chief=operations, ensign=any, medic=medical
weight: 3

= start
chief: Lock! I have a lock. Energising. | Signal's up. Locking on. Energising.
     | Combadge signal. Holding it. Holding. Energising now.
...
chief: Got them. Got them. They're aboard. | Materialised. We have them.
-> dazed 3, talk 2

= dazed
ensign: Where... is this the ship? Am I on the ship?
      | I was on a roof. I was on a roof a second ago.
      | Oh. Oh, it's so quiet. Is it over?

     | Easy. You're home. Sit before you fall down.
ensign: I can't stop shaking. | My hands won't stop. Sorry. Sorry.

     | Nothing to be sorry for. Breathe with me. In. Out.
-> ground 2, scan 1, who 1

= ground

      | I was in a school for a week. I heard them on the stairs every night.
      | I lost my tricorder in a river. I had to wait. Just wait.

      | I kept my combadge in my sock. Every night. In my sock.

     | The best thing you ever did. It got you here.
ensign: I'm sorry. I'm babbling. | I can't stop talking. Is that normal?

-> scan 2, who 1

      | I heard the chime. I didn't think it would work.

     | Scanning. Hold still for me, {ensign}.

     | Malnourished. A sprained ankle. You've been on it for days.
ensign: I'm fine. I'm fine. | Is that bad? | I ran on that ankle.
medic: You're not fine. You'll be fine. There's a difference.
     | You're alive. Everything else, we can fix.
-> who 2, sickbay 1

= who

      | Who was that? Someone came. They had a tricorder.

     | We never know. Just a signal, and a lock, and then you.
ensign: They didn't say a word. Just held up the tricorder and nodded.
      | I think I said thank you. I don't remember. I hope I did.

-> others 2, sickbay 2

= others

      | Did the others make it up? We got separated in June.
chief: Some. Not all. I'll get you the list when you've rested.
     | A few are aboard. Sickbay has the names. You'll see them.
ensign: Not all. | ... Some. Right.
...
medic: One thing at a time. You first. | Today, you. The rest tomorrow.
-> sickbay 2

= sickbay

     | Can you walk? Lean on me. It's not far.
ensign: Can I have a shower? A real one? | I'd kill for a bath.
      | Is there food? Real food? Not out of a tin?

     | Sonic first. Then Grieg's soup. It's very good soup.

     | Adding it to the list. Another one home.

     | I just pressed the button. The hard part was on the ground.

= chief_alone

     | Home. Good. Good.
-> end

# The transporter chief remembers every name.

scene: tr_every_name
where: transporter
cast: chief=operations, other=any
weight: 2

= start
other: You keep a list? | What's that padd you're always holding?
     | Is that the transfer log?
chief: Names. Everyone who's come up on this pad since February.
     | Everybody I've brought home. And the ones I haven't. Yet.
other: May I? | Can I see? | You don't have to show me.
-> list 3, memory 2

= list
chief: Novak. Came up bleeding and apologising for the carpet.
     | Tamura. Came up singing. Wouldn't say what.
chief: Haddad. Off a roof. Refused the medic. Fainted two steps later.
     | Sato. Asked if we still had coffee. First thing. Not hello.
other: You remember all of them. | You remember what they said.
chief: Every one. You don't forget the first thing a person says.
     | I'm not going to forget them. That's not how it works.
-> eleven 2, memory 1

= memory
other: How do you remember them all? | Doesn't it wear on you?
chief: It's not memory. It's the pattern. You hold a person, once.
     | Every pattern goes through the buffer. I watch every one.
chief: For four seconds, a whole person is numbers on my console.
     | For a moment you're responsible for every atom of someone.
other: I'd never thought of it like that. | That sounds heavy.
chief: It's the best job on the ship. | Heavy. And the best job there is.
-> eleven 2

= eleven
other: How many of the eleven? | And the surface team? The eleven?
chief: Eleven stayed for the fieldwork. Some are back. Some aren't.
     | Eleven chose to stay. I've brought up some of them. Not all.
chief: I keep the others at the bottom of the list. Blank line after each.
     | The ones still down there have a line each. Waiting to be filled in.
other: Brennan's on there. | Lindqvist. She was in my study group.
chief: Last heard on the twenty-ninth. | Beacon went quiet in June.
...
other: Do you think they're...? | Do you think...
chief: I think the line's blank. That's all I know.
     | Blank means not yet. I'm not writing anything else in it.
-> stayed 2, hope 1, others 1

= stayed
other: Can I ask why they stayed? | Why did they stay down, the eleven?
     | I was on nights in February. I never heard why the eleven stayed.
chief: Fieldwork. That's the answer everyone gives. It's true.
     | For the work. Every one of them. The survey wasn't finished.
-> e_work 3, e_argue 2

= e_work
chief: They were in the middle of it. Market days. Radio. The harvest.
     | Six months of study, half done. They wanted to finish it.
other: Even after the sabotage? | With the ship crippled?
chief: In February, the planet was having a very ordinary week.
     | The ship was stuck, not sinking. And the county was fine.
chief: We could beam them any time. They could reach us. So they stayed.
     | There was no reason to come up. The transporters worked.
-> e_june

= e_argue
chief: The captain argued. Came down here herself. Stood on the pad.
     | Vale stood right there and argued with them on an open channel.
other: And she let them stay? | She didn't order them up?
chief: She could have. She asked instead. She said it was their call.
     | She said the work mattered. She said please be careful.
other: That sounds like her. | That's Vale.
-> e_june

= e_june
...
chief: Then June. | And then late June. Two days.
other: Did you try to bring them up? | Couldn't you get them all up?
chief: I tried. For two days I didn't leave this room.
     | Every lock I could hold, I held. Some beacons, I couldn't find.
chief: The ones who weren't near a badge. Or who'd lost it running.
     | Some were too far. Some were underground. Some went quiet.
other: I'm sorry. | That wasn't your fault. | You did what you could.
chief: I know. I'll know it better one day. | Tren says the same.
chief: Since July, somebody's been finding them. One at a time.
     | And now somebody down there brings them. One by one.
-> hope 2, others 1

= others
other: What about the people who aren't ours? From the county?
     | Could you bring up someone from down there? A civilian?
chief: If someone put a signal on that pad, I'd take them.
     | The captain's ruled. Anyone who reaches us. No questions.
chief: They'd go on the list like anyone else. First words and all.
     | They'd get a line. Same as everyone. Same ink.
-> hope

= hope
other: I hope you fill them in. | I hope you get to write them all.
chief: So do I. | Every one of them.
     | Me too. I've got a very nice stylus saved for it.
other: Thanks for showing me. | Thank you. For keeping it.
chief: Somebody should. | Come by any time. I'll be here.
-> end

# Who is down there, beaming them up?

scene: tr_who_beams
where: transporter
cast: chief=operations, other=any, third=any
weight: 3

= start
other: Another one last night? | I heard the chime at two a.m.
     | Is it true there was another rescue?
chief: Ensign Moreau. Came up at oh-two-hundred. Sickbay has her.
     | Another ensign. Twenty minutes after the beacon lit.
third: That's the fifth this month. | That's quick work. Whoever it is.
-> who 3, theory 2

= who
other: So who's doing it? Who's down there?
     | Nobody knows who's answering the calls? Honestly?
chief: Honestly? No. A combadge in their hand, and that's all I see.
     | We get a lock on the ensign. Never on the one who found them.
third: The ensigns never get a name. A face, sometimes. A nod.
     | They say someone arrives, scans them and steps back.
-> theory 2, grateful 2

= theory
third: A survivor. A local who found a combadge. That's my guess.
     | One of the eleven, maybe. Someone we don't know is alive.
other: A local wouldn't know how to use a tricorder.
     | One of ours would've come up by now. Surely.
chief: Unless they don't want to. Shepard didn't, for months.
     | Maybe they're too busy. It's a big county.
third: Or somebody else entirely. | Or maybe it's somebody we'll never meet.
-> ask 2, kess 1, twice 1

= ask
other: What do the ensigns say about them? The ones who came up?
     | Did anyone describe them? Height, anything?
chief: Novak said they smelled of woodsmoke and had a very old tricorder.
     | Haddad said they wore something odd. Not a uniform. Maybe a uniform.
third: Sato swore they hummed the whole time. Badly.
     | Tamura says they talked to her. Kept her awake. Told her jokes.
other: That's not much to go on. | So a person. Probably human. Maybe.
chief: That's the most anyone's got. | It's a start.
-> kess 2, grateful 1

= kess
chief: Kess ran the numbers. Somebody's on the ground and on the shuttle.
     | Kess says it's the shuttle's signal every time. The same crew.
other: The shuttle? Shepard's shuttle? It's parked down there.
     | Somebody's using Shepard's shuttle? With the lamps?
chief: Somebody's living in it. Somebody's using the sensors.
     | Somebody found it and learned it. And now they answer our calls.
third: Shepard must know. | Does Shepard know? She must.
chief: If she does, she's not saying. | She smiles when you ask.
-> grateful 2

= twice
other: What if there's more than one of them? | Maybe it's a team.
chief: The pattern's different sometimes. The signal comes from new places.
     | Could be. Some nights two beacons. Some nights none.
third: A team of strangers saving our people. | That's a lot of strangers.
other: Strangers who know a tricorder. | Strangers with our equipment.
chief: Which is either very good or very strange. | I'll take it either way.
-> grateful

= grateful
...
chief: Whoever they are, I'd shake their hand. | I owe them. We all do.
other: I'd give them my holodeck time. A whole week of it.
     | I'd give them my replicator ration. All of it.
third: Grieg says she's saving a pie for them. A real one.
     | I'd just like to say thank you. To a face.
chief: They might come up one day. | Maybe they're listening.
     | Maybe they're standing in this room. Who knows.
other: Now you're being spooky. | You'd notice a stranger on this ship.
chief: Would I? | I'd like to think so.
third: I'll keep an eye out. Anyone who looks lost and competent.
     | That's half the ship. Lost and competent.
chief: Then I'll thank all of you, just in case. | That's the ship, yes.
-> end

# Fear of transporters.

scene: tr_fear
where: transporter
cast: nervous=any, chief=operations
weight: 2

= start
nervous: I don't have to go today, do I? | I'm on the roster for a test beam.
       | Is it true the survey team's using the pad today?
chief: Pad two, ten hundred, you and a crate of sample jars.
     | Your name's on Kess's list. Ship to cargo bay. Short hop.
nervous: Short. Right. | Fine. Great. Absolutely fine.
-> admit 3, deny 2

= deny
chief: You look pale. | Are you all right? You look like you ate replicator fish.
nervous: I'm fine. I love transporters. They're my favourite thing.
       | I'm perfectly calm. This is my calm face.
chief: Your hands are shaking. | You've sweated through your uniform.
nervous: It's warm in here. | That's... circulation.
-> admit

= admit
nervous: All right. I hate it. I've always hated it.
       | I don't like being taken apart. There. I said it.
chief: You're not the first. | A lot of people don't. More than you think.
chief: Doctor McCoy hated them. Barclay had the whole condition.
     | Transporter phobia is in every Starfleet medical text since McCoy.
nervous: That was nearly two centuries ago. | Barclay? He saw things in the beam.
chief: Some things don't change. | He did. He was fine. Mostly.
-> explain 2, joke 2

= explain
chief: Here's how it works. You stand. I lock. Four seconds. Done.
     | You won't feel a thing. A tingle. Like cold water on the arms.
nervous: And in those four seconds? | Where am I, for those four seconds?
chief: In my buffer. With me watching every atom. I don't blink.
     | Right here. On this screen. I've never lost anyone off this pad.
nervous: Never? | Not once?
chief: Not once. Not in nine years. | Not one person. I'd remember.
-> history 1, go 2

= joke
chief: Think of it this way. The version of you that walks off is brand new.
     | Philosophers have argued about that since Cochrane. Don't think about it.
nervous: That's not helping. | That is the worst thing you could have said.
chief: Sorry. Bad joke. Old transporter chief's joke.
     | Yes. Sorry. That one's for engineers.
-> explain

= history
nervous: What about the surface? When they beam up from the surface?
       | And people coming up from below? It's not like cargo.
chief: They're hurt, scared, with the dead a few metres off. And it works.
     | Harder. Weaker lock. Moving target. And it still works. Every time.
nervous: If they can do it from a rooftop... | Then I can do it to the cargo bay.
chief: That's the spirit. | Exactly that.
-> go

= go
...
chief: Pad two. Deep breath. | Up you get. Hands at your sides.
nervous: Can you tell me when? | Don't count down. Just do it.
       | Count me down. Slowly.
chief: Three. Two. | No countdown. Just... now.
-> after 2, bail 1

= after
nervous: Did it... oh. Oh, that's it? | I'm still here. I'm in the cargo bay.
       | That was it? That was nothing.
chief: That was it. Every atom present and accounted for.
     | Told you. Your nose is exactly where you left it.
nervous: I think I'm going to sit down. | I want to do it again. No. Never.
-> end

= bail
nervous: Wait. Wait. Can I take the turbolift? It's four decks.
       | Actually, I'll walk. I'll carry the crate. I love walking.
chief: It's thirty kilos of sample jars. | Take the lift. Next week, the pad.
nervous: Next week. Definitely next week. | I'll build up to it.
chief: Next week. I'll put you down. | I'm writing it in Kess's schedule.
-> end
