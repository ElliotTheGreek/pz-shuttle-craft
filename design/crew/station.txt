# The field station under the electronics store in Muldraugh (FIELD_STATION.md).
# Part of the eleven: the survey team who stayed on the ground, sealed the
# station when the county died at the end of June, raised the masking field
# and went silent. They watch the county on their screens, keep its records,
# and argue about whether to call the ship.
# CREW.md section 4 is the format; section 2 is what these people know, and
# FIELD_STATION.md 2 is what these people know that the ship does not.
# `station` is anywhere down here; `any` is the ship's, and never plays here.

# ---------------------------------------------------------------------------

weight: 4

= start

 | Have a look at camera four for me. By the post office.
 | {b}, come and look at this. Four. No, four.

a: Wait for it. There. Walking. | Give it a second. There, by the mailbox.

-> person 2, shape 2, glitch 1

= person
b: That's not a walk I know. That's a person. That's a living person.

a: Carrying something. A bag. Heading for the pharmacy.
 | They're checking the door. Nobody from out there checks a door.
b: Log it. Time, place, direction, what they're carrying.
 | Mark it. Every one of them gets written down.
a: And then? | And then what do we do about them?
-> nothing 2, argue 1

= shape
b: No. Look at the feet. That's one of them.
 | It's the shuffle. I'm sorry. That's one of them.
a: I was so sure. | It looked like it was going somewhere.
b: They all look like they're going somewhere. That's the worst of it.
 | Half of them walk the routes they walked before. To work. Home.
a: Mark it anyway? | Do we still log them?
b: We log everybody. They were people. They get a line in the book.
 | Yes. Name unknown, last seen outside the post office. Everybody.
-> quiet

= glitch
b: That's the feed. The camera on the water tower drops a frame.
 | That's static. The tower camera's been ghosting since Tuesday.
a: I could have sworn. | Are you sure?
b: I'm sure. I climbed up there to fix it in May. It did it then too.
 | Sure as I can be from under a shop. I'll go up and check it tonight.
a: Don't go up on your own. | Take somebody with you.
-> quiet

= nothing
b: What we always do. Nothing. We watch, we write it down.
 | Nothing. The masking field doesn't open for one person.
a: They're somebody's. They're alive out there on their own.
 | We have a replicator down here. We have a Doctor.

 | Then say it at the briefing. Say it louder than last time.
-> quiet

= argue
b: We've had this conversation. | Don't. Not today.
a: We have. And every time we lose a day. | Somebody has to keep having it.
b: If they see this place, whoever did this to the county sees it too.
 | One door opened is one door found. You know the rule.
a: The rule was written by people who weren't down here.
 | The rule didn't watch Muldraugh die on a monitor.
...
b: Write it down. That's what I can give you today.
 | Log it. We'll take it upstairs. Metaphorically.
-> quiet

= quiet
...
a: Back to it, then. | Right. Screen six. | I'll keep an eye on four.
b: Call me if they come back. | Shout if they move. | Good eyes, {a}.
-> end
