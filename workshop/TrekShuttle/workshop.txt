title=Star Trek: Starfleet Shuttlecraft (Build 42)
description=A Star Trek Starfleet Type 6 shuttlecraft and the starship U.S.S. Adirondack for Project Zomboid Build 42 -- single player, hosted co-op and dedicated servers.
description=
description=5 new weapons: Phaser (never runs dry, far quieter than a gun, cuts down trees and cuts through locked doors), Bat'leth, Mek'leth, Lirpa, Ushaan-tor -- plus the shuttle's photon torpedoes.
description=3 new medical items: Hypospray, Dermal Regenerator, Medical Tricorder.
description=18 new foods and drinks: Ration Pack, Gagh, Leola Root Stew, Plomeek Soup, Jumja Stick, Andorian Tuber, Oskoid, Wing-Slug Roll, Hasperat, Rokeg Blood Pie, Chadre'kab, Raktajino, Earl Grey, Romulan Ale, Bloodwine, Andorian Ale, Balso Tonic, Nutrient Suspension.
description=22 new farming items: seven alien seeds, their produce, dried and ground ingredients, and live serpent worms.
description=Plus a Tricorder, a PADD, six Starfleet uniforms and dilithium crystals.
description=
description=A SHUTTLE YOU CAN LIVE IN AND TRAVEL IN
description=A cabin with a helm, a working galley, running water, a biobed, a sick bay, a replicator, a warp core and Starfleet lockers.
description=- Right-click anywhere -> Shuttlecraft -> Beam up to the shuttle.
description=- At the helm, click the map to lay in a course, then Take her down. You are beamed to the site and she follows.
description=- Right-click open ground to call her down (she needs 3x5 clear tiles).
description=- The landed shuttle is a four-seat vehicle. From the pilot's seat, Take her up and fly her by driving. The pilot fires photon torpedoes: hold right mouse to aim.
description=- Everything she does runs on the dilithium crystal in her warp core. An on-screen gauge shows what is left; at zero she goes dark. Load crystals by right-clicking the core.
description=
description=THE U.S.S. ADIRONDACK
description=A working starship in orbit. Beam across from the shuttle and ride the turbolifts between five decks: the Bridge, the Lounge, Galley and crew quarters, the Transporter Room and Sickbay, Main Engineering, and Hydroponics. Fifty new pieces of Starfleet furniture -- seats, beds, doors that open as you walk up, and stocked lockers. She has a crew: officers of every division and several species ride the lifts, walk to their posts, sit, and talk about the ship, the county and you.
description=
description=HYDROPONICS
description=Grow seven alien crops on Deck 5 -- tea, bergamot, Klingon coffee, plomeek, leola root, Andorian tuber and hasperat peppers -- and cook Earl Grey, Raktajino, plomeek soup, leola root stew and hasperat from scratch. The ship waters and tends the trays; you sow and harvest. Feed the serpent worm tanks and they breed: five worms and a bowl make live gagh. Homegrown food is real food, which Klingons and Real Food Only characters will happily eat.
description=
description=THE PADD
description=Load books onto it at any library and read them five times faster than paper. Transcribe VHS tapes onto it. Open it with K. It is also how the Adirondack talks to you.
description=
description=THE STORY
description=Lt. Shepard calls your PADD from orbit. Fifteen threads tell what happened here and who made the county, with six holo fragments to find and put on tape.
description=
description=COLD START OR COMMISSIONED
description=Cold Start: the shuttle lands near you, dark and empty. Find the first crystal, bring her to life, learn the story, and work your way up to the Adirondack. Commissioned: she starts fully powered with spare crystals.
description=
description=THE REPLICATOR
description=Makes any item in the game -- vanilla, patches and other mods -- once the ship holds its pattern, paid for in dilithium energy. Scan what you carry to teach it. It cannot make dilithium.
description=
description=THE DOCTOR
description=An Emergency Medical Hologram treats what the hypospray and regenerator cannot, for energy. He is the only cure for a zombie bite: one whole crystal and twelve hours aboard.
description=
description=SPECIES, PROFESSIONS AND RANK
description=Play a Vulcan, Klingon, Andorian, Betazoid, Trill, Bajoran, Talaxian, Orion, android or liberated Borg -- each with its own strengths, price, look and home cooking -- or a human. Seven Starfleet professions: command, helm, engineering, security, medical, science and cultural survey, each with its own perk and kit. New traits: Transporter Phobia, Spacesick, Real Food Only, Starfleet Academy, Holo-historian. Rescue downed ensigns to rise from Ensign through Lieutenant (j.g.), Lieutenant and Lieutenant Commander to Commander.
description=
description=SHIELDS
description=Nothing dead gets within ten tiles of the landed ship. They are pushed back, not killed. Raise them at the helm.
description=
description=QUICK GUIDE
description=- The sensor console (aboard: Shuttlecraft -> Long-range sensors) launches probes and answers distress calls. Its map marks are approximate -- always take a tricorder.
description=- Probes find dilithium and holo fragments 80 to 260 tiles out. Go there and sweep with the tricorder: crystals show within twenty tiles.
description=- Dilithium lies on wild ground -- fields and woods, never in town.
description=- Distress calls: accept one at the sensor console and a life sign is marked on your map, up to forty tiles out. You have three days. Go there with a tricorder: the ensign shows as a blue cross within forty tiles. Right-click them and beam them to safety from within three tiles.
description=- Phasers: right-click a tree or a locked door to cut through it.
description=
description=MULTIPLAYER
description=Fully multiplayer compatible and server-authoritative: everyone shares one shuttle and one Adirondack. Dedicated servers: add the map folder in front of the base map in the server .ini -- Map=TrekShuttle;Muldraugh, KY. Single player and in-game hosting add it for you. The sandbox Shuttlecraft page covers access, transporter charges, torpedo fire, the replicator, the start mode, wild dilithium, the Doctor, hover height, first contact, phaser cutting and hydroponics.
