    /*  Contraband (CONTRABAND.md): what the off-watch crew keep in the
        hideouts off the Adirondack's Jefferies tubes, where the first
        officer will not look.

        **The drugs are Food.** Ketracel-white, felicium, Trellium-D and
        cordrazine are Food items with their own menu word -- Inject, or
        vanilla's Take -- and no hunger at all, which is exactly how vanilla
        makes antibiotics and cigarettes: the eating menu offers the word,
        vanilla's eating action runs, and its complete() runs Eat() on the
        server. TREK_Traits wraps that complete(), and the wrap hands the dose
        to TREK_ContrabandServer, which is where everything a dose does is
        decided -- the high, the habit, the withdrawal. The only effect the
        script itself carries is painReduction, which Eat() applies through
        BodyDamage.setPainReduction and nothing in Lua can reach as simply.

        **Nothing here can be replicated** (C.ReplicatorBlocked): the hideouts
        are the only place any of it comes from.

        Icons are generated on magenta and keyed (tools/gen_contraband_icons.py);
        the world and in-hand models are vanilla's, borrowed by name, which
        tests/test_assets.py checks.  */
