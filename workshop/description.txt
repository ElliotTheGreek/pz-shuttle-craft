A Star Trek Starfleet Type 6 shuttlecraft and the starship U.S.S. Adirondack for Project Zomboid Build 42: single player, hosted co-op and dedicated servers.

11 new weapons: Phaser and Phaser Rifle (never run dry, far quieter than a gun, cut down trees and cut through locked doors), Klingon disruptor and disruptor rifle, Romulan disruptor, Jem'Hadar polaron rifle, Cardassian phaser, Bat'leth, Mek'leth, Lirpa, Ushaan-tor, plus the shuttle's photon torpedoes and a Starfleet holster.
3 new medical items: Hypospray, Dermal Regenerator, Medical Tricorder.
18 new foods and drinks: Ration Pack, Gagh, Leola Root Stew, Plomeek Soup, Jumja Stick, Andorian Tuber, Oskoid, Wing-Slug Roll, Hasperat, Rokeg Blood Pie, Chadre'kab, Raktajino, Earl Grey, Romulan Ale, Bloodwine, Andorian Ale, Balso Tonic, Nutrient Suspension.
22 new farming items: seven alien seeds, their produce, dried and ground ingredients, and live serpent worms.
Plus a Tricorder, a PADD, six uniforms, a field pack, a shoulder lamp, perimeter phaser sentries, dilithium crystals and an engineer's field kit.

A SHUTTLE YOU CAN LIVE IN AND TRAVEL IN
A Starfleet cabin: helm, galley, running water, bunk, biobed, sick bay, replicator, warp core, armoury, and Lt. Shepard's 1993 TV.
- Once she knows you: right-click anywhere -> Shuttlecraft -> Beam up to the shuttle.
- At the helm, click the map to lay in a course, then Take her down. You are beamed to the site and she follows.
- Right-click open ground to call her down (she needs 3x5 clear tiles).
- The landed shuttle is a four-seat vehicle. From the pilot's seat, Take her up: W A S D fly her, Q and E turn her. Any seat fires: hold right mouse to aim.
- Everything she does runs on the dilithium crystal in her warp core. An on-screen gauge shows what is left; at zero she goes dark. Load crystals by right-clicking the core.

THE U.S.S. ADIRONDACK
A working starship in orbit. Earn your way aboard (bring her crew home, recover three pattern enhancers, hold a transporter lock and pass the Doctor's biofilter, or open her from the start in the sandbox), then ride the turbolifts between five decks, from the Bridge to Hydroponics. She has a crew: officers of every division and several species walk to their posts, sit, and talk about the ship, the county and you.

CAPTAIN TITUS
Sit down with the Adirondack's captain on her bridge and ask what happened here. She tells you what the ship knows as the story unfolds, and keeps one thing back until you have earned it.

JEFFERIES TUBES AND CONTRABAND
Crawl between the Adirondack's decks through Jefferies tubes, over the stars. Three hideouts off them hold what the off-watch crew keep from the first officer: ketracel-white, felicium, Trellium-D, the Ktarian game, latinum, and other cultures' weapons. Some of it is habit-forming; the Doctor will detox you.

THE FIELD STATION
Somewhere in Muldraugh a Starfleet survey station is hidden under an ordinary shop. Listen to the Adirondack's crew: they talk about the field office that went dark in June. Find the way down to its quarters, operations, galley, armoury, infirmary and reactor, and the people who never left.

STARFLEET AT HOME
Install a warp core, a replicator and an EMH station in any house from kits (right-click the kit: Install here). Load the core with dilithium and everything within 25 squares runs on it. The ship's replicator makes the kits. Dismantle packs a machine back up.

BUILD ABOARD
Build and place what you like in the shuttle, on the Adirondack and in the field station, and move their furniture like vanilla's. Anywhere else, phasers clear stumps, boulders and bushes, and the engineer's field kit's tools replace vanilla's, weigh less and never wear out.

HYDROPONICS
Grow seven alien crops on Deck 5 (tea, bergamot, Klingon coffee, plomeek, leola root, Andorian tuber and hasperat peppers) and cook Earl Grey, Raktajino, plomeek soup, leola root stew and hasperat from scratch. Feed the serpent worm tanks and they breed: five worms and a bowl make live gagh.

THE PADD
Load books onto it at any library and read them five times faster than paper. Transcribe VHS tapes onto it. Open it with K. It is also how the Adirondack talks to you.

THE STORY
Lt. Shepard calls your PADD from orbit. Fifteen threads tell what happened here and who made the county, with six holo fragments to find and put on tape.

THREE WAYS TO START
Marooned, the default: she is down, dark, somewhere across the county, and you have no Shuttlecraft menu and no beaming. Find an annotated map where maps are kept, reach her and walk aboard. Cold Start: dark, but beside you. Both: find the first crystal and bring her to life. Commissioned: overhead and powered. Choose on the sandbox Shuttlecraft page; existing saves are not affected.

THE REPLICATOR
Makes any item in the game (vanilla, patches and other mods) once the ship holds its pattern, paid for in dilithium energy. Scan what you carry to teach it. It cannot make dilithium.

RAIDS
When the transporter's pattern buffer overflows, Captain Titus calls for help. Accept and you are beamed to an outpost where the dead beam in round its warp core in growing waves, half running. Clear it, loot the crates, and go home.

THE DOCTOR
An Emergency Medical Hologram treats what the hypospray and regenerator cannot, for energy. He is the only cure for a zombie bite: one whole crystal and twelve hours aboard.

SPECIES, PROFESSIONS AND RANK
Play a Vulcan, Klingon, Andorian, Betazoid, Trill, Bajoran, Talaxian, Orion, android or liberated Borg, each with its own strengths, price, look and home cooking, or a human. Seven Starfleet professions: command, helm, engineering, security, medical, science and cultural survey, each with its own perk and kit. New traits: Transporter Phobia, Spacesick, Real Food Only, Starfleet Academy, Holo-historian. Rescue downed ensigns to rise from Ensign to Commander.

SHIELDS
Nothing dead gets within ten tiles of the landed ship: pushed back, not killed. Raise them at the helm.

THE MAP
Readable aboard, in her seats or with a tricorder or PADD, at any hour.

QUICK GUIDE
- The sensor console (aboard: Shuttlecraft -> Long-range sensors) launches probes and answers distress calls. Its marks are approximate: take a tricorder.
- Probes find dilithium and holo fragments 80 to 260 tiles out. Sweep there with the tricorder: crystals show within twenty tiles.
- Dilithium is rare: a town's loot holds one to five crystals, and a few lie on wild ground.
- Distress calls: accept one at the sensor console and a life sign is marked on your map. You have three days. Take a tricorder: the ensign is a blue cross within forty tiles. Right-click within three tiles to beam them to safety.

MULTIPLAYER
Everyone shares one shuttle and one Adirondack, and a server may give players a shuttle each. In a Marooned or Cold Start world she beams only players she has learned: walk aboard once. Dedicated servers: put the map before the base map in the .ini: Map=TrekShuttle;Muldraugh, KY.

FOR SERVER OWNERS (NEW IN 1.22)
Balance page: how rare dilithium is and what everything costs, as numbers you set (the defaults are hard: a crystal is about one outing). Content: switch off the shuttle, the Adirondack, the field station, aliens, any weapon or family of items. Roleplay: a shuttle each, who may be what, your own names.

LANGUAGES
All 27 of the game's languages, machine-translated.

REQUIREMENTS
Build 42. She needs 3x5 clear tiles to land.
