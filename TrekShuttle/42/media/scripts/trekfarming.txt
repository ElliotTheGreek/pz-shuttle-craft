        them, what the dehydrator and the kitchen make of it, and the recipes
        that end in the species dishes the mod already has -- Earl Grey,
        raktajino, plomeek soup, leola root stew, hasperat, roasted Andorian
        tuber and gagh.

        Every step is a vanilla mechanism: crops are vanilla farming crops
        (server/TREK/TREK_Farm.lua), the pots cook on a stove like vanilla's
        rice pot, the beans roast in an oven because they are cookable, and
        the recipes are craftRecipes, which show in the crafting window by
        themselves. Recipes are not gated: anybody can cook from scratch.

        Icons are generated on magenta and keyed (tools/key_icon.py). */
