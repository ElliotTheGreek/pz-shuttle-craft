/*  Shuttlecraft -- traits and professions (TRAITS.md).

    Every id here is registered in media/registries.lua; a block naming an
    unregistered id stops the scripts loading.

    Costs are vanilla's scale: a species costs roughly what its granted
    vanilla traits and boosts would cost bought one at a time, less what its
    drawback is worth. XPBoosts and GrantedTraits are the engine's own and
    need no code; everything else a trait does is a hasTrait() in
    TREK_Traits.lua or TREK_TraitsServer.lua.  */
