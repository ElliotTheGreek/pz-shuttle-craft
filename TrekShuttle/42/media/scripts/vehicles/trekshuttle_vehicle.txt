/*  The shuttle as a vehicle: four seats, driven like a truck.

    Module Base, like every working build 42 vehicle mod, so the vanilla part
    templates (Seat, Tire, Engine, ...) resolve without qualification.

    Deliberately doorless. A seat with no door part cannot be reached by the
    dead (AttackVehicleState only bites through an open door or a broken
    window), which is the safety hands-on flight never had. Vanilla's enter
    and exit menus assume doors in a few fallback paths; TREK_VehicleMenu.lua
    guards those for this vehicle only.

    Units: model scale 1, so every offset and extent is in tiles. The hull mesh
    (models_X/TREK_Shuttle.x) is 3.0 wide, 2.18 tall, 5.0 long, nose towards
    +Z, which is the vehicle's forward. Keep extents length at 5.0: the seat
    chart art is projected from the mesh at that length
    (tools/gen_vehicle_assets.py) and tests/test_assets.py checks it.

    The wheels are real -- a vehicle drives on its wheels -- and invisible:
    their model is TREK_Empty. They hang below the chassis, so the hull rides
    half a tile off the ground, which reads as hovering.
*/

module Base
