    /*  The engineer's field kit (FIELD_KIT.md): a satchel and five Starfleet
        tools, and the stem bolts that stand in for nails.

        **Each tool stands in for vanilla's by its tags.** Vanilla's recipes
        ask for a tool by tag (`tags[base:hammer]`, 140 of them; saw, wrench,
        screwdriver and the rest the same), so a tool carrying the tag is that
        tool to every recipe and every build. Nails and the blowtorch are the
        two things vanilla asks for by item id instead ([Base.Nails] 110 times,
        [Base.BlowTorch] 65), which no tag can answer; TREK_FieldKit adds the
        stem bolts and the welder to those recipes' own item lists when the
        world loads.

        **Unlimited uses** is the phaser's rule (TREK_PhaserCharge): the
        server puts a carried tool's condition and the welder's charge back,
        and ConditionLowerChanceOneIn is high besides, so wear between passes
        is rare. They are all lighter than what they replace.

        Meshes, texture and icons: tools/gen_fieldkit.py. The tools are held
        the way vanilla's hacksaw and blowtorch are: no hand attachment, the
        grip at the origin, the length up +Y.  */

    item TrekFieldKit

        ItemType = base:container,

        Icon = TREK_FieldKit,
        Weight = 0.5,
